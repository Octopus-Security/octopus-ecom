# Shorts cut from a video you made

<!-- Generated from server/playbooks/definitions.js by server/playbooks/render-md.js. Edit the definition, not this file. -->

**When to use:** A long video you are willing to sign is exported, and you are cutting one Short from it.

A Short is a cut of a video you already made, posted on the same channel, to bring a viewer to the long video or the listing. It is not a separate account and not a pile of other people's clips.

verified 2026-10-10 - https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 Compilations of other people's clips with no narrative are reused content. A Shorts-feed note published by a YouTube employee on 2026-10-01 said recommendation would favor original work and show less of re-uploads that add nothing (assumed, unverified as a permanent policy: https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/470890423). Your Short uses your voice and your picture.

verified 2026-10-10 - https://www.youtube.com/creators/partner-program/ Shorts views and long-form watch hours are different doors. A Short does not add watch hours toward the 4,000. Do not expect shop Shorts to unlock ads unless the Shorts door itself gets to 10 million qualified views in 90 days. The shop Short's job is the click to the listing. The story Short's job is the click to the long video.

SHAPE. 20 to 45 seconds, vertical. The length cap is assumed, unverified; if Studio rejects the file, use the cap it shows. First frame is the product or the story's conflict. Captions burned in. One idea. End on the next action. No duet of a stranger's video. No gameplay you do not own, including a three-second sample: a sample of the game is still the game. No commercial song as the sound.

## Steps

1. [ ] **Start from an exported long video whose rights.txt is complete. No Short from a video you would not sign** (`master`)
2. [ ] **Cut 20 to 45 seconds at the strongest beat, 9:16, captions burned in** (`cut`)
3. [ ] **Upload it to the same channel as the master** (`same`)
4. [ ] **Story Short links to the long video. Shop Short links to the live listing. Put that URL in the description and the pinned comment** (`point`)
5. [ ] **Set the same synthetic-content answer as the master** (`disclose`)
6. [ ] **One Short per long video. A second cut only if the first sent people after 7 days** (`count`)
7. [ ] **Use the master's licensed audio or the audio library. Do not attach a commercial song** (`audio`)
8. [ ] **Write the Short URL next to the master in that channel's LOG.md** (`log`)
