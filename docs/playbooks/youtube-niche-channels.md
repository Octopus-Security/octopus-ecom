# YouTube channels: one account, one niche each

<!-- Generated from server/playbooks/definitions.js by server/playbooks/render-md.js. Edit the definition, not this file. -->

**When to use:** You are opening the shop channel, the story channel, or a later niche.

One Google account, a separate channel per niche, each with its own name. verified 2026-10-10 - https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1646861 A personal channel is created from Settings, then Add or manage channels. A channel under a business or other name is created from the channel list (https://www.youtube.com/channel_switcher) and that flow creates a Brand Account, which can have more than one manager. Use the Brand Account path so the shop name is the channel name, not your personal name.

OPEN IN THIS ORDER
1. Shop. The name is the shop, or a plain design name you can say. Its job is to show real products and send people to a listing. Playbook: niche-shop-traffic. This one can earn before any ad program.
2. Story. Fiction only. Playbook: faceless-story-channel.
3. Explainer. One topic you can teach without pretending to be a lawyer, a doctor, or a financial adviser. Playbook: niche-explainers. Open it only after the first two channels have four public videos each. A third empty channel splits the week.
4. No fourth channel until one of the three has either sales or a month of work you would repeat.

A different niche means a different promise in About. Shop: designs you made, and where to get them. Story: original short fiction. Explainer: the topic sentence. If two channels would accept the same upload, they are one channel. Copying one template onto several channels is the mass-production case. verified 2026-10-10 - https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 says a new channel opened to get around a demonetization or a termination can cost you every channel.

ACCOUNT. Use the Google account you already control. Turn on two-step verification before you apply to the partner program; the creators page lists it as a requirement. verified 2026-10-10 - https://www.youtube.com/creators/partner-program/ A second Google account is for a real second person, not a spare in case of a strike. Passwords stay in the password manager. They do not go in this app, in a playbook, or in ~/octopus-content.

ABOUT. Say who makes the videos. Story says the stories are fiction. Do not write that you read Reddit. Do not put a game title in the name or the banner. Banners use art you own.

## Steps

1. [ ] **Sign in to the Google account you will keep, and confirm two-step verification is on** (`login`)
   verified 2026-10-10 - https://www.youtube.com/creators/partner-program/
2. [ ] **From the channel list, create the shop channel as a Brand Account: shop name, handle, a picture you own** (`shop-ch`)
   verified 2026-10-10 - https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1646861
3. [ ] **Create the story channel the same way, with a different name and handle** (`story-ch`)
4. [ ] **Write both About boxes. Shop names the shop. Story says original fiction** (`about`)
5. [ ] **Banners use your own art or a still you made. No game screenshot and no trademark** (`banner`)
6. [ ] **Use the channel switcher once before the first upload, so a story does not land on the shop channel** (`switcher`)
7. [ ] **Leave the explainer channel until shop and story each have four public videos** (`later`)
8. [ ] **If any channel is demonetized or terminated, stop and read the notice. Do not open a replacement** (`strike`)
   verified 2026-10-10 - https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
9. [ ] **Write the channel URLs in ~/octopus-content/CHANNELS.md. Nothing else about the Google account** (`record`)
