# Shop-traffic videos that sell a live listing

<!-- Generated from server/playbooks/definitions.js by server/playbooks/render-md.js. Edit the definition, not this file. -->

**When to use:** A product is live, or will be today, and you are making the video whose job is the sale.

This channel earns by sending a buyer to a listing. A view that buys is worth more than a view that might pay ad rates in a year. The partner-program rules still apply once you want ads: the videos have to be yours, and they have to differ. verified 2026-10-10 - https://www.youtube.com/creators/partner-program/

ONE VIDEO, ONE PRODUCT. Show the design large in the first three seconds, say who it is for, show it on the real product type, say that you designed it and a print partner makes it, and put the listing URL in the first line of the description and in the pinned comment. If the listing is not live, do not publish the video.

DO NOT
- Use a brand, team, character, or lyric in the design or the title. Same bar as the Etsy launch playbook.
- Call it handmade by you when a partner prints it.
- Stuff the title with a game name or any unrelated viral words. That brings a viewer who will not buy, and misleading metadata is a community-guidelines problem.
- Read a script that would fit any product. Name this design.

CADENCE. One video per design you will stand behind, plus one Short cut from it. A weekly roundup is a second format, and only after four live listings.

MEASURE. ~/octopus-content/shop/LOG.md: date, video URL, listing URL, views after 7 days, listing sessions if Etsy shows them, orders. After five videos, make more of the format that produced a session. Stop the one that did not. The views-no-sales playbook is for the listing once the listing itself has views.

The app still does not write an AI disclosure onto the Etsy listing. If a frame or the design is AI-made, you say so in the description and on Etsy.

## Steps

1. [ ] **Open the listing URL and confirm the price matches ecom. If it does not load, stop** (`live`)
2. [ ] **Write a 45 to 90 second script: who it is for, what is printed, who prints it, where to buy it. Save it under ~/octopus-content/shop/<slug>/** (`script`)
3. [ ] **Record the mockup from the product drawer or a flat lay you made. The product fills the frame in the first three seconds** (`shoot`)
4. [ ] **The voice says you designed it and a print partner makes it, matching the listing** (`say-partner`)
5. [ ] **Description line 1 is the listing URL. Line 2 is one sentence. No other shop's link** (`desc`)
6. [ ] **Pin a comment with the same URL** (`pin`)
7. [ ] **Cut one Short from the same master the same day, using the Shorts playbook** (`short`)
8. [ ] **Log the video URL, the listing URL, and a 7-day check in ~/octopus-content/shop/LOG.md** (`log`)
9. [ ] **At five videos, compare listing sessions. Repeat the kind that sent people. Stop the kind that did not** (`judge`)
10. [ ] **If any frame or the design is AI-generated, say so in the description and on the Etsy listing** (`disclose`)
